ITEX 2.0

Eheliyagoda National College

Welcome to ITEX 2.0, the premier technology and innovation exhibition hosted by the ECC ICT Society. This event marks a milestone in student-led technological advancement at Eheliyagoda National College, showcasing cutting-edge projects, interactive exhibits, and the brilliant minds of our student body.

This immersive 3D map was developed exclusively for ITEX 2.0 to help visitors, students, and parents navigate our campus in a futuristic, engaging way. By combining modern web technologies with precise geographical data, we aim to provide a seamless digital experience that reflects the spirit of our exhibition.
